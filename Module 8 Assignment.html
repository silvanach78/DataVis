Module 8 Applied Analysis: Dashboard for Managerial Decision-Making

You are preparing for a meeting with senior leadership at a mid-sized company that is reviewing employee data at the end of the fiscal year. Leadership is evaluating potential changes to:

  • overtime policies
  • compensation structures
  • workforce management

Using the same dataset from Module 6, your task is to create a decision-focused dashboard that communicates the most important findings clearly and supports managerial decision-making.

Download dataset (CSV)

Your Task:

Using the same dataset and analysis from Module 6, create a dashboard that communicates the most important insights for leadership.

Part 1. Dashboard Tool

You must use a tool that allows you to share an interactive dashboard. Tableau Public is preferred because it is free and easy to share, but you may also use Power BI or another dashboard application of your choice, as long as you can share the actual dashboard through a link.

Part 2. Dashboard Content

Your dashboard must include 2 to 4 visualizations based on the same dataset used in Module 6.

  • Each visualization must support a specific insight
  • Your dashboard should not include all of your Module 6 charts
  • You should select only the most important findings for leadership
  • Your visualizations must focus on variables that are relevant to key performance indicators (KPIs)

Part 3. Refinement

At least one visualization must be improved or redesigned from Module 6 to make it more effective for a non-technical audience. You may improve clarity, labeling, layout, focus, or the way the message is communicated.

Part 4. Dashboard Design

Your dashboard should be organized clearly and designed for quick interpretation by senior leadership.

  • Use a clear layout that guides the viewer's attention
  • Emphasize the most important information
  • Avoid clutter, unnecessary decorations, and excessive charts
  • Focus on decision-relevant metrics rather than trying to show everything

Part 5. Executive Summary

Write a short explanation of 150 to 250 words intended for senior leadership. Your explanation should:

  • Summarize the key insights shown in the dashboard
  • Explain what those findings mean for the organization
  • Provide a clear recommendation
  • Communicate the results clearly without overstating what the evidence shows

Submission Requirements:

  1. A shareable link to your interactive dashboard (Tableau Public, Power BI, or another dashboard tool)
  2. A Word document or PDF containing your executive summary
