Module 6 Applied Analysis: Framing Evidence-Based Requests

You manage the HR team at a mid-sized company reviewing employee data at the end of the fiscal year. Senior leadership is evaluating potential changes to:

  • overtime policies
  • compensation structures
  • workforce management

In preparation for the meeting, you will analyze the attached data to develop an evidence-based recommendation that supports managerial decision-making by identifying patterns that are relevant to key performance indicators (KPIs).

Download dataset (CSV)

Your Task:

Using the dataset provided, develop an evidence-based request using the structure below.

Part 1. Claim

State the main conclusion you want leadership to accept.

Part 2. Evidence

Create 3 visualizations to support your claim. These visualizations are intended to help you identify and support insights, not to serve as final presentation visuals. Your visualizations must include:

  • At least one relationship analysis (e.g., overtime vs. performance, satisfaction vs. turnover)
  • At least one segmented comparison (e.g., by department or job level)
  • Coverage of multiple variables related to KPIs
    Your charts should not all focus on the same variable. You must include at least two different outcome variables (e.g., performance, job satisfaction, supervisor support).

Part 3. KPI Alignment

  • Identify which variables are directly relevant to the KPIs
  • Explain how your evidence connects to those KPIs

Important: Analyses that rely on variables not clearly tied to KPIs will not receive full credit.

Part 4. Implication

Explain why your findings matter: What do the patterns suggest about performance, cost, or employee experience?

Part 5. Recommendation

Provide a clear, actionable recommendation:

  • What should leadership do?
  • Why does the evidence support this action?

Part 6. Prioritization

Briefly explain:

  • Which variables you focused on and why
  • Which variables you excluded and why they are not relevant

Submission Requirements:

  1. A word document with a short write up addressing all parts listed above (400-600 words total)
  2. Your Excel file with the 3 charts; or your syntax and output if your results were computed in R.
